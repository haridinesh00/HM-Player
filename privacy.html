Privacy Policy for HM Player

Effective Date: April 14, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the HM Player mobile application ("App"). The App is designed to function as an offline, local music and media player. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

2. Information Collection and Use

HM Player is a standalone, offline application. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, user behavior, or media files with external servers. All operations performed by the App, including reading audio files, managing playlists, and editing equalizers, happen entirely locally on your device.

3. Permissions Required

To function correctly as a music player, the App requires specific device permissions:

  • Storage / Read Media Audio Permission: The App requires read-only access to your device's local storage solely for the purpose of locating, indexing, and playing your audio files.
  • Foreground Service / Notifications: The App uses background services to ensure uninterrupted audio playback while the screen is locked or while you are using other apps. This requires displaying a persistent media control notification.

We do not use these permissions to scan your device for non-audio files, nor do we upload or transmit your files to the internet.

4. Third-Party Services

HM Player does not use any third-party tracking services, analytics tools, or external databases that secretly collect user information.

5. Security

Because all your data and media files remain strictly on your local device, the security of your data is governed by your device's own operating system and security settings (such as screen locks and device encryption).

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone. As an offline media player, it is safe for all ages, provided the user has the legal right to access the media stored on their device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
